About me

I'm a data engineer at Verbalyze, an AI voice-bot analytics platform for BFSI debt collection. I work on the part most people don't see: getting call data in, cleaning it, structuring it with LLMs and making sure it can be traced and audited later.

I like work where the data has to be right. That means schemas that hold up under load, pipelines that recover when something fails, and privacy controls built in from the start, in line with DPDP and GDPR principles.
